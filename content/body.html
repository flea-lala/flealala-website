Welcome To The Flea

Welcome Patna's Happiest Flea Market

The Flea La La is a festive shopping, food and music festival coming to Patna — in the joyful spirit of India's big-city flea markets, with colourful stalls, string lights, live sounds and finds you will not get in a mall.

Across two days, 40 curated brands, local makers and women entrepreneurs bring fashion, food, home decor, gifts and so much more to The Flea La La. Come with family, come with friends — join us for the fun.
